# Business and personal-brand workspaces

Signup asks whether the user wants to **Grow my business** or **Build my personal brand**. This preference is saved on the user and restored at login. Existing users default to business. It supplies the new-workspace default; each workspace separately stores its purpose, allowing one account to manage both kinds. Existing workspaces and approved drafts are preserved.

Personal onboarding asks for a name/brand, niche and expertise, target audience, goal and target. It does not require a company or problem/solution pitch. Initial outcomes are relevant followers, newsletter subscribers and qualified inquiries/opportunities. These cover audience, professional visibility and potential consulting/sponsorship leads without equating attention with revenue. Existing business onboarding retains its first-50-paying-customer goal.

Personal dashboards show the chosen metric and target, not the customer-sales funnel. Audience & opportunities lets editors record the current total with optimistic version checks. It is explicitly self-reported; automatic platform attribution, newsletter integrations and verification of opportunity quality remain follow-up work. Connected social account snapshots stay separate from manual goal totals. Reloading restores saved progress, and other tenants cannot read or modify it.

The same approved profile, interview/HeyGen demo, social connections, research confirmation, content review and routine controls are shared. Personal workspace prompts prioritize the chosen audience/opportunity outcome and do not impose a paying-customer target. Company-problem validation is skipped for personal research, and the product-launch agent returns no product-launch tasks. No virality, follower growth, opportunities or revenue is guaranteed. Instagram still requires a professional Business/Creator account regardless of the workspace's personal-brand purpose.

Manual advanced tools retain their original standalone business metrics; they do not feed personal-brand goal progress. Live avatar rendering and unattended publishing retain their existing implementation limits.
