# Platform growth intelligence

Content studio contains **Platform growth intelligence**, with source-linked guidance for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn, proposed experiments and metric limitations. This is public knowledge and test design, not reverse engineering private ranking models or a guarantee of virality.

The initial catalog cites official Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn publications, with explicit curated review dates and versioned source IDs. It does not claim those pages were fetched live. Account summaries report actual saved retrieval timestamps and available counts; ungranted follower totals, retention, shares-per-reach and professional analytics remain unknown. Refresh platform evidence reloads saved account snapshots, not live social metrics. Account retrieval is a separate owner action.

Every new automatic research run, including enabled daily monitoring, makes four additional bounded Brave searches on official publication domains for ranking/recommendation updates. This raises the maximum to fourteen search calls/results per run (eleven without optional competitors), with extra provider costs but no extra workflow reservation. Recent search results are unverified excerpts, not confirmed platform policy changes; no fresh result is fabricated when none is found. Weekly search freshness can miss older relevant updates. Niche trend/news/buyer research continues alongside these checks.

New research artifacts include four platform strategies: published ranking summary, a proposed experiment, success metric, evidence IDs and uncertainty. Sources are validated against the frozen run evidence/catalog; missing platforms or invented citations reject the artifact. The source catalog and research artifact participate in the exact founder-confirmation hash before content generation. Later agents receive these reports alongside the selected business or personal-brand goal. Legacy reports remain readable.

Experiments are proposals: compare one variable at a time, normalize metrics when denominators are available, and track the selected business/personal outcome separately. There is no automatic causal A/B evaluation or persistent self-training in this increment. Current calendars/publishing adapters retain their existing channel support; four-platform guidance/connections do not imply that automatic four-platform publication is implemented.
