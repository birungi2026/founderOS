## Work intentionally not sold as complete

TikTok/LinkedIn automatic video publishing, full paid-ad management, native app-store releases, automatic sales/refund verification against each customer's payment processor, AI ranking-algorithm reverse engineering, guaranteed virality, automatic follower authenticity certification and a fully autonomous video editor are not part of the validated first Instagram/Facebook pilot. Existing limited/manual tools are retained rather than relabeled as these capabilities.

## Paid pilot acceptance protocol

1. Interview ten coaches/instructors and ten personal creators. Ask to see the last post they produced, abandoned drafts, tools, actual spending, editing time, who they want to reach and what happened after publication. Do not treat compliments or a waitlist as purchases.
2. Recruit one narrow cohort for a clearly described assisted offer: a weekly plan and three finished posts, $59/month, with explicit video limits. State where editing/posting is assisted manually.
3. Capture a completed baseline window and existing promotion/collaboration activity. Assign immediate/delayed groups deliberately; the app records the assignment but does not claim randomization automatically occurred.
4. Check sources/brand facts before generating; review assets before scheduling. Test a professional Instagram account and a Facebook test Page first. Confirm public provider IDs/permalinks and compare returned metrics with the native dashboards.
5. Record publication, editing time, inquiries, paid promotion and a permitted random audience sample weekly. Unknown counts remain blank. Compare equal non-overlapping windows; net follower totals include unfollows. Small cohorts cannot establish strong causal effects.
6. Suggested internal gates: five purchases from fifteen qualified prospects; 70% of delivered assets actually published; median review/editing under twenty minutes/week; six of ten initial subscribers renew. These are proposed business thresholds, not industry benchmarks or statistical significance.
7. Record actual provider invoices, setup fees, support time and acquisition costs. Treat avatar training as upfront cash expenditure; do not amortize it using assumed retention. Decide whether to continue, narrow or change the offer based on renewals and contribution margin.
